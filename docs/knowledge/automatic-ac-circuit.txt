# Automatic A/C Circuit — Knowledge Document

**Vehicle:** 2020 Jeep Compass Altitude (2.4L Eng)
**Source:** ProDemand wiring diagram manual, "AIR CONDITIONING" section, *Automatic A/C Circuit* Fig. 1–5 of 5
**Related but distinct circuit:** *Manual A/C Circuit* (Fig. 6–10 of 5 in the same manual) — same power/relay layout but omits the Sun Sensor, In-Car Temperature Sensor, and Auto-Headlamp signal, since a manually-operated system doesn't need those automatic-climate-control inputs.

> **Note on accuracy:** This document was compiled from an OCR'd scan of a multi-page fold-out wiring diagram. Wire colors, component names, connector/splice IDs, and general signal flow are transcribed as printed. Exact pin numbers on the 30-way HVAC Module connector could not be perfectly aligned from the scan and should be treated as approximate — always confirm exact pin location against the original diagram or a factory pinout tool before probing/repairing.

---

## 1. System Overview

The Automatic A/C system is built around the **HVAC Module** (center of dash), which:

- Is powered by the **Blower Motor Relay** and **A/C Clutch Relay** (both inside the Power Distribution Center).
- Talks to the rest of the vehicle over **CAN IHS** (interior high-speed bus).
- Reads six temperature sensors, a sun-load sensor, and door position feedback to automatically drive three blend/mode/recirculation doors and the blower motor.
- Commands the A/C compressor clutch, which is ultimately grounded/switched through the **Powertrain Control Module (PCM)** and the **A/C Compressor Solenoid**, with pressure feedback from the **A/C Pressure Transducer** and a hard interlock via the **Series/Parallel Radiator Fan Relay**.

```mermaid

    PDC["Power Distribution Center<br/>Fuse 83 (40A) / Fuse 19 (7.5A)"] --> BMR["Blower Motor Relay"]

    FBMPM --> BLWR["Blower Motor"]
    ACR --> HVAC["HVAC Module<br/>(Center of Dash)"]
```

## 7. A/C Compressor & Cooling Fan Interface

- **A/C Compressor Clutch** (mounted on the A/C compressor): grounded/energized through `DK BLU/YEL` from splice `G904` (left side of engine).
- **A/C Compressor Solenoid** (left side of engine): 3-pin — `FUSED IGN` (PNK/GRN), `LIN BUS` (WHT/BLU), `GND` (BLK) — controlled via LIN bus, gated through Fuse 9 (7.5A) and the Series/Parallel Radiator Fan Relay.
- **Radiator Fan Module** (behind left side of upper grille): supplies `RAD FAN CTRL`, `RAD FAN ENABLE SIG`, `LOW RAD FAN RLY CTRL`, `GND` to the PCM, and receives Fused B(+) from Battery Distribution Unit Fuse A (50A).
- **PCM inputs relevant to A/C**: `A/C CLUTCH CTRL`, `A/C PRESSURE SIG`, `5V SPLY`, `SENSOR GND`, `ECT SIG`, plus `CAN C (+)/(−)` to the Computer Data Lines System.

## 9. Cross-Referenced Systems

- **Computer Data Lines System** — CAN IHS (HVAC Module ⇄ BCM) and CAN C (PCM ⇄ Radiator Fan Module / cluster).
- **Cooling Fans System** — Series/Parallel Radiator Fan Relay, Radiator Fan Module, Radiator Fan Resistor 1.
- **Engine Controls System** — ECT sensor, A/C pressure transducer, A/C compressor solenoid, all terminating at the PCM.
- **Body Control Module** — supplies switched power/ground to ambient and duct temperature sensors, hood ajar switch, and humidity sensor (LIN bus); relays Auto Headlamp signal to/from HVAC Module.

## 10. Quick Diagnostic Notes

- No blower operation: check Fuse 83 (40A) and Blower Motor Relay before condemning the Front Blower Motor Power Module.
- No compressor clutch engagement: check Fuse 19 (7.5A) and A/C Clutch Relay at the HVAC Module side, then verify PCM `A/C CLUTCH CTRL` and A/C Compressor Solenoid wiring (LIN bus circuit) and A/C Pressure Transducer signal (PCM won't engage clutch outside pressure range).
- Erratic auto-climate temperature control: verify In-Car Temperature Sensor and Sun Sensor circuits first (unique to Automatic A/C — absent on Manual A/C variant), then the four duct/ambient sensors routed through the BCM.
- Door not responding (blend/mode/recirc): confirm shared Reference Voltage Supply and Sensor Ground at the HVAC Module before replacing an actuator.
